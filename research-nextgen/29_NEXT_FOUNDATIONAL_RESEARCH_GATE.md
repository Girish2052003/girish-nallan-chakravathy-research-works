# Research continuation gate after NG-Q12 / NG-Q13 (1 November 2026)

**Purpose:** Preserve the exact mathematical stopping point and prevent an autonomous continuation from rediscovering already-rejected variants. This is not a new proposed KEM or an assertion that a novel structure has been found.

## Established findings to preserve, not erase

- NG-Q1..Q3: additive public-shift quotient attack and counterexamples; random source-form ensembles often have no nontrivial shifts.
- NG-Q4..Q9: finite-field shift/collision first moments and model-specific high-overlap **polynomial-time public planted-witness recovery** under precisely independent source-factor sampling.
- NG-Q10: sparse two-anchor star factors are standard bounded-treewidth public CSPs, solvable in deterministic polynomial explicit-table time.
- NG-Q11: ternary d=1 source factors are affine for **any** global overlap graph; r=1,d=2 local scalar tables are low degree and publicly distinguishable from uniform random tables.
- NG-Q12: full 27-entry public local table allows **equivalent r=1,d=2 generator recovery** by 11232 GL3 basis checks. A fixed B,A yields only 729 distinct source forms, each with 27 potential-table representatives. No original-secret uniqueness is asserted.
- NG-Q13: apparent secret-permutation nonassociativity of a group isotope is publicly normalizable into an elementary abelian group and yields full equivalent secret permutations from the complete public Latin-square table. The Fano Steiner quasigroup is *not* group-isotopic but public division is still trivial.

## Mandatory candidate-definition and adversarial evaluation gate

**A — Precise asymptotic mathematics.** Define a sequence of finite objects X_n, exact operations, domains/codomains, representations and public-input sizes polynomial in n. State exact evaluation/verification costs and scaling. A fixed 27-entry table or fixed 7-element quasigroup is not an asymptotic cryptographic foundation.

**B — Genuine mathematical non-equivalence.** Record explicit maps/reductions to or separations from: linear/group transport and isotopy, finite-field polynomial isomorphism and equivalent keys, Reed–Muller degree spaces, finite-difference images, standard CSP propagation/treewidth, presheaf/local-to-global cohomology, and known quasigroup/loop classifications. A new name or nonassociativity alone is insufficient. Any proposed invariant must have a proof or a rigorously tested finite counterexample to known constructions.

**C — Meaningful nontrivial mathematics.** A candidate must state nontrivial theorems whose hypotheses matter, include proofs, smallest counterexamples/negative controls, exact machine tests and remaining proof obligations. Distinguish original statement from standard methods and comprehensive novelty uncertainty.

**D — Independent public-hardness challenge.** Define a public search/decision problem with correct instance sampler, fixed planted-witness independence and relevant security parameters. Challenge with generic algebraic systems, public linearization/degree analysis, equivalent-coordinate recovery, CSP propagation/graph decomposition, known-isomorphism attacks and quantitative resource bounds. The fact that a relation has a unique satisfying witness, high treewidth or few additive shifts is *not* evidence of one-wayness.

**E — Actual private advantage before KEM.** Only after (A)-(D) survive, define a secret trapdoor with experimentally and theoretically justified private inversion advantage over the public representation. Prove correctness, key distribution and a suitable security reduction; conduct quantum-relevant analysis before claiming post-quantum security. No parameters get a fabricated 128/192/256-bit label. If evidence fails, reject and record, rather than cosmetically modifying the same object.

## Highest-value next experiment (not already done)

Look for **asymptotically scalable**, succinctly represented mathematical relations with a distinct local-to-global obstruction **not equivalent** to an ordinary CSP witness problem or known group-isotopy/IP disguise. The first concrete deliverable should be a finite definition with operations and a rigorous prior-art equivalence/rejection table, **not** an encryption demo. Research into non-group-isotopic operations should explicitly test whether inversion remains public via known row/column algebra, group translation structures or general generic solvers. A Fano-plane-sized example cannot establish a one-way family.
