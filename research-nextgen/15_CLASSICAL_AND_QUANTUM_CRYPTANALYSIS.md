No `B_i`, `A_i`, `Q_i` generation secrets or planted witness enter the attack algorithm. No hypothetical KEM security games are claimed or simulated.

## Originality/independence boundary

A different AI code path and brute-force cross-checks are useful internal challenge but do not constitute independent external expert cryptanalysis. ## NG-Q8 — asymptotically proved public one-pass attack on the exact tested source distribution

[Full mathematical argument](23_NGQ8_PUBLIC_RECOVERY_THEOREM.md) and [public-only algorithm](experiments/ng_q8_recovery.py). The local false-pair rejection probability is 15/52 (survival 37/52) for uniformly randomized F3 source-form factors with r=1,d=2, independent Q and B. Complete 3-uniform scopes yield public planted-s recovery with probability at least `1-min(1,8*C(n,2)*(37/52)^(n-2))` in polynomial explicit-input time. This **disproves high-probability planted-witness one-wayness** for this distribution, rather than merely showing finite toy attacks.

The new one-pass algorithm is weaker at finite small n than iterative NG-Q7: tests recovered n=6:0/12; n=12:0/10; n=20:1/5; n=30:2/3 in separate deterministic campaigns. The theorem's union bound is trivial at n<=20 and nontrivial at larger n. **No result is a KEM IND-CPA/IND-CCA break**, as no completed active NCSG-only KEM exists; no quantum lower or upper bound or external cryptanalysis is claimed. This is a stronger **classical public-recovery upper bound** for specified sampled mathematical instances.


## NG-Q9 wider classical public-recovery theorem

[Full result](24_NGQ9_GENERAL_ARITY_PROJECTION_BOUND.md). For any fixed r,d>=1 with k=r+d, independently sampled public source-form local maps over F3 with complete local tables, a **strictly positive exact lower bound** epsilon_(r,d) on wrong-pair rejection yields a polynomial-time one-pass public-recovery theorem whenever pair codegrees scale sufficiently. For k>=3 complete k-uniform scopes meet the sufficient asymptotic condition; k=2 needs multiple independent constraints per pair. The numerical lower bound can be extremely conservative, and it is not a finite-parameter security estimate, quantum attack lower bound or KEM break.

## 9. Quantum-query and implementation-complexity correction (2 November 2026)

**Classical versus quantum access:** The bound Pr[identify uniformly random needle u after q classical membership queries] <=(q+1)/s^L is **CLASSICAL**. For a coherent quantum oracle implementing f_u, standard Grover search can find the marked u among N=s^L candidates in O(sqrt(N))=O(s^(L/2)) quantum queries. This is asymptotically optimal for unstructured search under the usual oracle model (C. H. Bennett, E. Bernstein, G. Brassard, U. Vazirani, *Strengths and Weaknesses of Quantum Computing*, SIAM J. Comput. 26(5), 1510–1523, 1997, DOI 10.1137/S0097539796300933; Christof Zalka, *Grover's quantum searching algorithm is optimal*, Physical Review A 60, 2746, 1999, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.60.2746). The present project did NOT implement Grover or quantum-counting controls; the quantum bound is external published prior art, not a new proof.

**A true computational-public-key model is still absent.** A public inspectable implementation of f_u must be produced without disclosing u, or else the oracle hardness model is irrelevant to the key's security. Replacing a freely inspectable public program with an inaccessible black-box membership oracle changes the problem definition. Do not infer 128-bit post-quantum hardness by taking L=256, nor infer IND-CCA/QROM KEM security.

**Reference code caveat:** the small Python validator `is_prime` uses trial division; the reconstructed linear algebra is polynomial field arithmetic given an already validated F_p, but the entire demonstration implementation is NOT proven polynomial in log(p). The exploratory basis finder is deliberately exponential in d. These are two distinct complexity caveats.

**Novelty remains rejected:** all core finite Hankel-rank and automata reconstruction techniques have 1971–2025 prior art, and the needle is an elementary black-box adversarial control. NG-C17 cannot pass M2, C2 or C3.
