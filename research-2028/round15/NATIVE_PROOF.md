# A shorter differential KEM under the same decoding assumptions

The sender transmits fewer ternary coordinates. The receiver still recovers every honest message, including when all the errors fall within the transmitted portion. The proof maps the new public-key and payload games to the **original Round 14 decoding distributions at their original dimensions**.

## Dimensions and wire protocol

Take a [Round 14 source layout](../round14/PARAMETERS.md). Use $m_0,t_0,N_0=m_0+t_0$ for its base, check and total coordinate counts, $h$ for its linear masking dimension, $n$ for the quadratic inputs, $d=h+n(n+1)/2$ for its row count, and $w,z$ for the secret check and noise weights. The source leaves $r=m_0-(t_0-1)w$ positions available for each secret check after conditioning on the others. Write $t$ for the new [concatenated code](OUTER.md)'s encoded trit count. Use the following construction when $t\le t_0$:

$$m=r+(t-1)w=m_0-(t_0-t)w,\qquad N=m+t\le N_0.$$

The compressed base still fits all $t$ disjoint checks because $m-tw=m_0-t_0w\ge0$. The noise sampler still uses the larger $N_0$. The protocol parameters, including the source outer-code identifier, determine the hash suite.

1. Sample independent public and private seeds. Regenerate a ternary matrix $A$ with $d$ rows and $m$ columns from the public seed. Regenerate $t$ mutually disjoint signed weight-$w$ checks $S_j$ on its $m$ coordinates from the private seed. The public wire contains a 32-byte public seed and the check columns $A S_j$. Pack the check coefficients in row order, grouping at most 64 consecutive rows in each canonical base-three rank. The recipient stores the two seeds.
2. The sender samples a uniform message of the source bit length. For conditional IND-CPA first consider independent base coins. Sample $v=(u,(x_i x_j)_{i\le j})$ as in Round 14. Independently sample a signed weight-$z$ vector $E$ on **all $N_0$ positions**, then keep only its first $N$ coordinates via $\pi$. With $M=[A\mid AS_1\ldots AS_t]$, the ternary body is
   $$Y=M^\top v+\pi(E)+(0^m,C(b)).$$
   The native signal is the finite difference in the clock direction of $P(s,u,x)=s M^\top v$. The source's joint rank, message, noise and sign conventions are retained. The independently randomized base ciphertext also has a public uniform nonce.
3. The receiver evaluates the residuals $Y_{m+j}-S_j^\top Y_{1:m}$. For the KEM, independent hash domains bind the canonical public wire and the complete suite to the sender's message. Hash $G$ supplies both the nonce and base randomness. Hash $H$ supplies the 32-byte shared key. The recipient decodes a candidate, reconstructs the public key from its seeds, re-encapsulates that message deterministically, and compares the entire ciphertext. Failure gives an explicit rejection. [Reference implementation](projected_kem.py)

**Perfect honest agreement.** For a noiseless transmission each residual equals its code coordinate because $(AS_j)^\top v=S_j^\top A^\top v$. A corrupted base position affects at most one residual by disjointness. A corrupted check position affects only its own residual. Since $\operatorname{wt}(\pi(E))\le z$ on every source coin tape, the new [decoder's radius-$z$ guarantee](OUTER.md) recovers the sender's message. The nonce and the recomputed body agree byte-for-byte. This argument holds for every key generated by the real samplers.

## Distribution-preserving reductions

Take $\epsilon_1$ as the maximum raw gap of a quantum distinguisher in the original iid $d\times r$ signed weight-$w$ syndrome game. Take $\epsilon_2$ as the corresponding gap in the original iid $h\times N_0$ generator-matrix, weight-$z$ noisy-word game. These bounds refer to the simulation's actual resource use and independent oracle access. [Round 14 definitions](../round14/BASE_PROOF.md)

**Public key.** For exact iid $A$ and exact private ranks, replace the $t$ public check columns one at a time with independent uniform columns. To simulate either side of one transition, pick the other $t-1$ signed checks first, with dummy supports for any already replaced columns. Their complement has exactly $m-(t-1)w=r$ coordinates. Put the old challenge matrix into those coordinates of $A$ and fill the others with fresh independent trits. Use the supplied challenge syndrome for this check column. A planted syndrome embeds a uniform signed weight-$w$ vector on the complement. A uniform syndrome gives the next hybrid. The remaining real check columns are computable. Thus the cost in success probability of the $t$ hybrids is at most $t\epsilon_1$.

**Payload.** After the column replacements the full $d\times N$ public matrix is iid. Given an original challenge $(G,W)$ with $G$ of size $h\times N_0$, take the first $N$ columns as the linear rows of $M$. Sample the quadratic rows independently. Restrict $W$ to its first $N$ coordinates. If $W=G^\top u+E$, then
$$\pi(W)=G_{[:,1:N]}^\top u+\pi(E).$$
If $W$ is uniform and independent, its restriction is uniform and independent of the public rows. Add the simulated quadratic row contribution and the encoding of the selected challenge message. This gives exactly the required distribution in each case. The ciphertext positions in the shortened matrix are interpreted using the new base/check split. The payload simulation occurs after key replacement, when all columns are iid. It does not require deleting coordinates of an honest old public key.

A uniform challenge bit now has guessing success $1/2$ in the final hybrid. The raw two-message base gap before finite sampling is at most $2t\epsilon_1+2\epsilon_2$. Sampling a fresh weight-$z$ error on $N$ positions would change the error law, since the implemented projection has weights from zero through $z$ when enough positions are omitted. Keeping the original space in the implementation is essential.

**Finite samplers, seeds and chosen ciphertexts.** The source joint encryption rank gives statistical distance $\nu\le2^{-\sigma}$ from ideal with body slack $\sigma$. Deterministic projection cannot increase that distance. Each matrix row uses an independent framed ideal hash output reduced modulo $3^m$ with $s_A$ slack bits. As in the [conditioned-lift argument](../round14/SEEDED_MATRIX.md), program each of the $d$ target addresses with a uniform lift of its exact uniform row rank. Every other address remains independent. The entire ideal table changes in total variation by at most $d2^{-s_A}$. The embedding knows the whole shortened matrix before running the adversary. Since the canonical wire is reconstructible from the seed and the check columns, binding that wire in $G,H$ is valid for both hybrids. The randomized PKE gap for the seeded-matrix, exact-check hybrid is at most
$$\epsilon=\min(1,2t\epsilon_1+2\epsilon_2+2\nu+2d2^{-s_A}).$$

The uniform nonce gives spread at least its bit width $s$. Apply the [explicit-reject FO theorem and advantage conventions](../round14/CCA_PROOF.md) with this $\epsilon$ and zero correctness error. For $q=q_G+q_H$, combined hash-query depth $D$, at most $q_D$ adaptive **classical** decapsulations and message width $\ell$, the raw KEM gap before changing exact checks is bounded by
$$
8\sqrt{(D+q_D)\epsilon}+16(q+q_D)2^{-\ell/2}
+48q_D(q_G+4q_D)2^{-s/2}.
$$
Allow the earlier-query correction $2q_{\rm pre}2^{-\ell}$ if applicable. In the fourth independent random-oracle domain, let $q_S$ be the number of private-expansion queries, $\kappa$ the entropy in the hidden seed and $s_S$ the per-check slack. The [hidden-seed hybrid](../round14/PRIVATE_SEED.md) adds $4q_S2^{-\kappa/2}+2t2^{-s_S}$ after the transform. In that argument, a tester supplied with the whole tape can generate the public key and answer every legal decapsulation using its known checks. Clip the final raw bound at one. With no decapsulation queries this gives the KEM IND-CPA guarantee. This is a single-recipient proof against quantum computation with classical ciphertexts and decapsulation access. The proof idealizes the SHAKE256 domains. [Hövelmanns and Majenz, Theorem 1](https://eprint.iacr.org/2023/1811.pdf)

Here $s_A=s_S=\ell+64$, $\kappa=8\lceil\ell/8\rceil$ and the three named suites retain the original message and nonce widths. The number of public-check hybrid terms decreases from $t_0$ to $t$. The 192-target [sufficient conditions](../round14/PARAMETERS.md) consequently remain sufficient if the same bounds hold at the new reductions' actual time, memory and oracle resources. The old [witness and cubic differential reductions](../round14/WITNESS_PROOF.md) apply to the same two source distributions. Their concrete hardness remains open.

## What the projection saves

The implementation streams matrix rows and reads each packed 64-row check block as needed. The receiver still constructs the public wire to replay FO. The reference is variable time.
