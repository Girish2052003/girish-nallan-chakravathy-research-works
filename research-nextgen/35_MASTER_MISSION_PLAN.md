## 6. Mandatory candidate and theorem record template

For EVERY serious candidate: define objects/elements/representation; parameter domain; exact operations; axioms and model consistency; invariants; local/global or composition relations; decision/search/inversion/equivalence problems; input size, verification and asymptotic cost; nontrivial examples; known equivalent structures, prior art and reductions; cryptographic motivation; public and private attacker models; honest rejection status.

Any found equivalence, mathematical contradiction, public inversion, lack of private advantage, or broken reduction triggers REJECT/REVISE/OPEN. No cosmetic repackaging.
